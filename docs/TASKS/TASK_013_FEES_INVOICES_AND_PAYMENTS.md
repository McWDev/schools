# Task 013 — Fees, Invoices, Receipts, and Balances

## Objective
Track school fees and student balances accurately.

## Scope
Fee structures, invoices, payments, receipts, discounts/waivers, arrears, payment methods, reconciliation fields, and finance reports. Confirm currency, numbering, and rules before coding.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Money values use precise decimal/numeric database types, not floating-point arithmetic.
- [ ] Payment creation is transactional and protects against duplicate submission.
- [ ] Receipts have controlled unique numbers within the agreed scope.
- [ ] Refunds and corrections are permissioned and audited.
- [ ] No payment card details are stored directly.
- [ ] Financial exports are tenant-scoped and permissioned.
