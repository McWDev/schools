# Task 017 — Notifications and Messaging

## Objective
Deliver controlled announcements and operational notifications.

## Scope
In-app notifications first; email/SMS/WhatsApp providers only after vendor and cost decisions. Recipient groups must be scoped by school, branch, class, and verified guardian relationships.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Recipients are calculated server-side.
- [ ] Users cannot spoof sender identity or recipient scope.
- [ ] Delivery failures are visible to authorized operators.
- [ ] Logs do not expose message secrets or unnecessary student details.
